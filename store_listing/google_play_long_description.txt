Planned Maintenance is an offline reference for planned maintenance on 2D laser systems and supporting equipment. Use a guided lookup to find task requirements, service intervals, estimated time, and maintenance-sheet references from bundled schedule data.

FIND THE MAINTENANCE INFORMATION YOU NEED
- Browse tasks by equipment group and follow the fields that appear
- Narrow each choice step by step; values with only one match are filled in automatically
- Review task descriptions and estimated durations
- Check required personnel levels, out-of-service status, and maintenance-sheet references
- See schedule, time-in-service, checking, replacement, and as-needed intervals
- Learn about L1 operator, L2 maintenance technician, and L3 authorized service technician levels
USE IT OFFLINE
The maintenance schedules and level descriptions are included with the app, so you can browse them without an internet connection. The app offers system, light, and dark appearance modes, plus in-app guidance for using the schedule and understanding its fields.

SUPPORT
You can send a question to the support service from the Information tab. Sending requires an internet connection and submits the name, email address, and question you enter, along with app version/build, package, platform, orientation, and layout information. Do not include sensitive information in a support request.

SAFETY
This app is a reference aid, not a replacement for the machine manual, approved maintenance sheets, qualified training, lockout procedures, or workplace safety requirements. Always follow current approved instructions and ensure work is performed by appropriately qualified and authorized personnel.
